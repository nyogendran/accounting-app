# BO-06: Routine accounting work automated, with people deciding the exceptions

Proposed by the requirements engineer in WF-01M5D0SE8MMS39CTPTXH8ME1HT step 4. The product owner
decides. Nothing below this line is normative.

## Evidence in SRC-001

- "automates the accounting workflow end to end" (SRC-001 p.4, overview, S1-0-c01)
- "A user creates the schedule once and does not raise a journal again for that item." (SRC-001 p.30,
  §2.2.7, S1-2.2.7-c01)
- "The following are generated and posted by the system on a schedule rather than raised by hand"
  (SRC-001 p.65, §2.6.12, S1-2.6.12-c01)
- "A line coded by hand once can be turned into a rule, so the same payment never needs coding
  twice." (SRC-001 p.60, §2.6.3, S1-2.6.3-c02)

## Indicators

Both indicators come from SRC-001 but carry bracketed values. They are written here with the
brackets kept, and are not targets until the product owner confirms them.

## Relation to the assistants

SRC-001 also specifies assistants that propose but never act ("It posts nothing, approves nothing,
releases nothing", S1-2.1.9-c02, p.20). This objective covers rule-based automation that SRC-001
says the system performs; the assistants serve BO-05 and are phased later.

## Value to the first customer

Tiqri's finance team spends its time on the bank lines, suspense items and exceptions that need a
person, not on routine coding and journals.
