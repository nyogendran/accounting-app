# BO-02: Every figure evidenced and traceable to its source

Proposed by the requirements engineer in WF-01M5D0SE8MMS39CTPTXH8ME1HT step 4 and revised in step 6
after REV-01M5EP1W4DYPM707EB79AYEYV1 (finding 11(c)). The product owner decides. Nothing below this
line is normative.

## Evidence in SRC-001

- "Every transaction carries a complete audit trail, supporting transparency, compliance and audit
  requirements." (SRC-001 p.4, overview, S1-0-c03)
- "Audit trail — user, time, before and after values, and the supporting document — attached at every
  stage above, permanently" (SRC-001 p.4, overview figure, S1-0-c05)
- "Supporting evidence — the scan, the approval and the calculation stay with the transaction, not on
  a shared drive" (SRC-001 p.4, overview figure, S1-0-c06)
- "Every figure in the financial statements must be traceable, on screen, to the document that
  supports it and to the working that produced it. This is a functional requirement of the system,
  not a filing habit." (SRC-001 p.98, §2.11, S1-2.11-c01)
- "Every create, amend, delete, approve, reject, cancel, export and login event is written to a log
  that no user or administrator can edit." (SRC-001 p.10, §2.1.4, S1-2.1.4-c01)
- "Drill-down from any statement line to account, journal, source document and attached scan, in no
  more than four clicks." (SRC-001 p.36, §2.2.10, 4, S1-2.2.10-c04)

## Indicators

Version 1 had two indicators that measured the same thing: the four-step limit of S1-2.2.4-c32 and
the four-click limit of S1-2.11.7-c01. SRC-001 states that limit three times (S1-2.2.4-c32,
S1-2.2.10-c04, S1-2.11.7-c01); version 2 keeps one of them. The other two indicators measure the
other halves of the statement:

- the uneditable audit trail, by the acceptance test S1-2.1.11-c02 (p.22);
- the completeness of the evidence, by the audit-pack test S1-2.11.7-c05 (p.100), whose sample size
  is a bracketed value.

All three are SRC-001 acceptance tests, and all three are in release 1 of the proposed phasing. The
executive dashboard has its own, longer limit of six steps from a KPI (S1-2.15.11-c01), which is used
under BO-05.

## Value to the first customer

Tiqri's finance team and its auditor answer a query about any figure from the system, without a
shared drive, an email search or a paper file.
