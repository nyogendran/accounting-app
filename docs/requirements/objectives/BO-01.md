# BO-01: One ledger, posted automatically from the source transaction

Proposed by the requirements engineer in WF-01M5D0SE8MMS39CTPTXH8ME1HT step 4. The product owner
decides. Nothing below this line is normative.

## Evidence in SRC-001

- "a single centralised platform on which the entity records, processes and reports every financial
  transaction" (SRC-001 p.4, overview, S1-0-c01)
- "Each transaction is validated, classified and auto-posted to the general ledger through predefined
  accounting rules" (SRC-001 p.4, overview, S1-0-c02)
- "Each module raises its own accounting entries as its transactions are approved, and the manual
  journal is the only other way into the ledger. Nothing is re-keyed between modules" (SRC-001 p.7,
  §1 The system in one picture, S1-1-c16)
- "The general ledger is the single accounting repository. Every module posts into it automatically
  from the transaction that gave rise to the entry; nothing is re-keyed, and no module keeps a second
  set of balances of its own." (SRC-001 p.26, §2.2.4, S1-2.2.4-c01)
- "Every sub-ledger transaction generates its ledger entries automatically at the moment it is
  approved." (SRC-001 p.27, §2.2.4 Posting controls, 1, S1-2.2.4-c14)

## Indicators

Both indicators are SRC-001's own: the nil sub-ledger agreement in 2.12.1 and the acceptance test in
2.2.4. Neither carries a bracketed value, so neither needs a value confirmed. The second indicator
names payslips, depreciation runs and stock issues, so it is met in full only once payroll, fixed
assets and inventory are delivered (see `docs/requirements/release-phasing.yaml`).

## Value to the first customer

Tiqri's finance team keeps one set of books with no re-keying between systems and no month-end
re-agreement of sub-ledgers by hand.
