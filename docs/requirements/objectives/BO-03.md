# BO-03: Controls that protect every published figure, workable by a small team

Proposed by the requirements engineer in WF-01M5D0SE8MMS39CTPTXH8ME1HT step 4 and revised in step 6
after REV-01M5EP1W4DYPM707EB79AYEYV1 (finding 10). The product owner decides. Nothing below this
line is normative.

## What changed in version 2

Version 1 said an entry becomes a record of account "only when a second person approves it". Read
literally, that forbids posting by rule or schedule, which SRC-001 requires:

- a bank coding rule's "Behaviour Auto-post, or propose and hold for confirmation — set per rule"
  (SRC-001 p.61, §2.6.5, S1-2.6.5-c06), with rules "created and amended under maker–checker"
  (S1-2.6.5-c08) and "Posting rule creation and amendment Entity — maker–checker always" (p.67,
  S1-2.6.16-c08);
- "The following are generated and posted by the system on a schedule rather than raised by hand"
  (p.65, §2.6.12, S1-2.6.12-c01);
- a schedule "the system posts on its own. A user creates the schedule once and does not raise a
  journal again for that item" (p.30, §2.2.7, S1-2.2.7-c01).

For these the approval sits on the rule or the schedule, not on each entry. Version 2 says so,
without stating an approval route that SRC-001 does not give: for posting rules SRC-001 states
maker–checker; for schedules it states a preview "confirmed before anything is committed" and a
posting behaviour of "auto-post on the scheduled date, or generate a draft for release by [role]"
(p.30, S1-2.2.7-c11, c12). Who confirms a schedule, and the "[role]" that releases drafts, are open
(workbook).

Version 1 also said an approved entry "is never edited", which took the side of S1-2.2.6-c10 against
S1-2.2.6-c14 on dimensions. Version 2 states only what both clauses agree on (value, account, date,
party; no deletion) and links Q-007.

## Evidence in SRC-001

- "Approval is the point at which an entry becomes a record of account. Before it, the entry is
  working material and can be changed. After it, the entry is fixed: it cannot be edited and it cannot
  be deleted by any user, including an administrator." (SRC-001 p.28, §2.2.6, S1-2.2.6-c01)
- "Transactions post only into periods the entity has authorised, and a period that has been reported
  on cannot change without that change being visible. Period control is the mechanism that protects
  every figure already published." (SRC-001 p.31, §2.2.8, S1-2.2.8-c01)
- "Two controls are enforced by the system itself and cannot be switched off by any role
  configuration: a user cannot approve a document they raised, and no role can amend or delete the
  audit log." (SRC-001 p.9, §2.1.2, 5, S1-2.1.2-c16)
- "so that a small finance team with one qualified person is not blocked from operating" (SRC-001 p.9,
  §2.1.2, 6, S1-2.1.2-c17)
- "Nothing is paid that has not been matched and approved" (SRC-001 p.48, §2.5, S1-2.5-c01)

## Open questions that shape this objective

- Q-007: whether the dimensions of an approved entry may change (S1-2.2.6-c10 against c14; qualified
  accountant).
- Q-009: how preparing, reviewing and approving a reconciliation are separated, and whether a small
  team can release that separation (qualified accountant).
- Q-013: what the override route into a hard-closed period may be used for (qualified accountant).

## Value to the first customer

Tiqri's finance team can show its auditor that no approved figure has changed silently, while a small
team still closes its books without breaking a control.
