# BO-07: One configurable platform that grows by entity, country, currency and module

Proposed by the requirements engineer in WF-01M5D0SE8MMS39CTPTXH8ME1HT step 4; version 2 (step 8)
adds the dependency of the third indicator on decision D-2.1.7-c84, after
REV-01M5EQY9R7BC1SY2XDY6S9YA52. The product owner decides. Nothing below this line is normative.

## Evidence in SRC-001

- "The system is one platform whose functions are switched on per entity. An entity takes only the
  modules its operation needs, configured for the way its industry works, and adds more later without
  disruption to what is already running." (SRC-001 p.13, §2.1.7, S1-2.1.7-c01)
- "The platform supports growth from a single standalone module, to a single-entity accounting
  deployment, to a multi-entity, multi-jurisdiction environment, without changing product and without
  a re-implementation at any step." (SRC-001 p.17, §2.1.7 Scalability and expansion, 3, S1-2.1.7-c81)
- "Industry-specific workflows and processes are delivered through configuration, never through a
  change to source code, so two customers in different sectors run the same released version."
  (SRC-001 p.15, §2.1.7 Industry configuration, 1, S1-2.1.7-c53)
- "The tax set is a property of the entity, not of the software. Adding a country, a tax or an
  exemption is configuration." (SRC-001 p.68, §2.7, S1-2.7-c07)
- "The system supports trading, holding balances and reporting in any currency" (SRC-001 p.34, §2.2.9,
  S1-2.2.9-c01)
- "Users in different countries operate the system in their own language against one centralised
  accounting database." (SRC-001 p.17, §2.1.8, S1-2.1.8-c01)
- "No country's rules are written into the software." (SRC-001 p.90, §2.10, S1-2.10-c02)

## Why this matters for the phasing

This objective is what makes a phased delivery safe: release 1 can serve one Sri Lanka entity, and
later releases add countries (Norway, Q-003), entities and modules as configuration and activation
rather than as rework. It also bears on architecture: the tech lead decides how the release 1 design
keeps later countries, languages and entities open (see the risks in the phasing file).

## Value to the first customer

Tiqri's finance team starts in Sri Lanka with LKR and other currencies, and adds Norway and other
countries later (Q-003). The same platform then serves other tenants (Q-001).
