# Q-055: Two meanings of "adjustment journal"

**Question.** Is the "adjustment journal" that corrects an approved entry (2.2.6) the same journal type
as the "Adjustment" journal of 2.2.5, which is raised in the year-end adjustment period and approved by
the Finance manager? Or is it a correcting entry in the current open period, approved by any user other
than the preparer?

**What SRC-007 says.**

- "Adjustment | Raised in the period's adjustment period for closing or audit adjustments, reportable
  separately | Finance manager" (p.37, S7-2.2.5-c05).
- "A correction is made by a new entry — a reversal, an adjustment journal, a credit note or a debit
  note as the case requires — which references the original document number, carries its own reason,
  and is approved by someone other than the person raising it" (p.38, S7-2.2.6-c11).
- "The correction is dated in the current open period" (p.38, S7-2.2.6-c12).
- "Entry in a closed period | Dated adjustment journal in the current open period, reported as a
  prior-period item" (p.38, S7-2.2.6-c16).
- "Adjustment period | An additional period dated at the year end, outside the twelve, for closing and
  audit adjustments" (p.40, S7-2.2.8-c04).

**Why it matters.** The two readings give a different period, approving role, number series (journals
are numbered by journal type, S7-2.1.10-c04) and reporting. The foundation increment's journals
(GOV-SCOPE-INCR-001 v1, CAP-12) cannot be specified until this is settled.

**Open.** The accounting meaning is the qualified accountant's; the scope effect is the product
owner's (DEC-0010 condition 5). Raised from REV-01M61MRRKX52P8XHG3NZXSPCZJ finding B2. The page
numbers come from the clause index; these passages were checked against the text extraction, not the
rendered PDF.
