# Q-020: Statutory retention periods per country

**Question.** What are the statutory retention periods for each record type in Sri Lanka and Norway?

**What SRC-001 says.**

- Six clauses say "retained for [7] years" (audit log, transaction history, fixed assets, payroll, reconciliations, group records).
- S1-2.11.4-c05 makes retention configurable, "not a fixed figure in the software" (p.100): the longer of the entity's own policy and each jurisdiction's statutory period.

**Why it matters.** The default policy and whether one model covers all records are for the product owner (Q-025).
