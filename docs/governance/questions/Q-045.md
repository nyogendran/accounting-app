# Q-045: Opening bank balances where bank is Enforced

**Question.** How is an opening bank balance loaded where bank control is Enforced?

**What SRC-007 says.**

- Bank is Enforced even in the lightest sub-ledger default ("Advisory, and Enforced for bank", p.27, S7-2.2.1-c59), so a manual journal to it is refused.
- For an uploaded chart, "Opening balances load against it under 2.16.2" (p.26, S7-2.2.1-c36). Whether that route serves a delivered chart, or an opening balance that is not migrated data, is not stated.

**Why it matters.** The first entity needs its opening bank balances on day one (with Q-006 and phasing D-2.16.2).

**Note** (2026-10-28). Q-033 v2 records an answer so far that bank starts at Advisory, not Enforced. If
confirmed, an opening bank balance can be keyed as a manual journal with a reason and appears on the
exception report, and this question becomes how opening balances should be loaded rather than how
they can be.

**Open.** Decision by the qualified accountant. Source: GOV-IMPACT-SRC007 v2, PQ-16.
