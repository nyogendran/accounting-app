# Q-019: Customer tax registration status against registration number

**Question.** Which governs whether a customer is registered for tax: the status field or the presence of a number?

**What SRC-001 says.**

- The customer master holds a mandatory registration status that drives the document type (S1-2.3.1-c05), and also says "the presence of a number is what marks the customer registered" (p.36, S1-2.3.1-c06). Nothing says what happens when the two disagree.

**Why it matters.** It is the attribute that drives tax determination on every sales document.

**Open.** Decision by the tax specialist. Source: REV-01M5DADKQY1VNR1FMN2414KZ58.
