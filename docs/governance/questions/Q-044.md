# Q-044: Location on balance-sheet accounts

**Question.** Is location mandatory on every transaction, or blocked on most balance-sheet accounts as SRC-008's chart sets it?

**What SRC-007 says.**

- The dimension model makes location apply to "All transactions" (p.31, S7-2.2.3-c06). SRC-008's chart blocks location on most balance-sheet accounts.

**Why it matters.** It sets the dimension rules of the delivered chart.

**Open.** Decision by the qualified accountant. Source: GOV-IMPACT-SRC007 v2, PQ-15.
