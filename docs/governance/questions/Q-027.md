# Q-027: Two cross-reference errors in SRC-001

**Question.** Confirm the reading of two cross-references that point to the wrong section.

**What SRC-001 says.**

- "The person responsible for reconciling it under 2.10" (p.24, S1-2.2.2-c13) points to Payroll. The rule is in 2.12: "Every balance sheet account is assigned an owner" (p.101, S1-2.12.3-c01).
- Budgets are cited to 2.2.3 (S1-2.13.1-c14, S1-2.1.7-c46), which has no budget clause. Budgets are specified in S1-2.2.10-c03 and 2.14.11.

**Assurance recommendation** (REV-01M5DADKQY1VNR1FMN2414KZ58, E4, advisory only): Read the first as 2.12, and the second as 2.2.10 and 2.14.11. SRC-001 itself is never edited.

**Why it matters.** Editorial only; no accounting decision is needed.

**Open.** Decision by the product owner. Source: REV-01M5DADKQY1VNR1FMN2414KZ58.
