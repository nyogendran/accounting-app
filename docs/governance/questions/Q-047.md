# Q-047: Manual journals below Enforced against the nil agreement

**Question.** If manual journals reach control accounts at Not applied or Advisory, which governs: the levels, or the rules that sub-ledgers agree to their control accounts?

**What SRC-007 says.**

- "These agreements are auto-computed by the system continuously and must be nil" (p.114, S7-2.12.1-c01).
- "The receivables control account equals the customer ledger continuously, and no balance in it exists without a document behind it" (p.45, S7-2.3-c01).

**Why it matters.** A manual journal to a control account creates a difference between the ledger and the sub-ledger.

**Open.** Which clause governs is a scope decision for the product owner; the accounting effect is the qualified accountant's. Source: GOV-IMPACT-SRC007 v2, PQ-18.
