# Q-041: Accounts in the spare range

**Question.** What class, natural balance and statement does an account in the spare range take?

**What SRC-007 says.**

- "A spare range is reserved outside the five classes" (p.25, S7-2.2.1-c30), but the class "determines the natural balance and the statement the account appears in" (p.24, S7-2.2.1-c03). SRC-008 sets the spare range at 690000 to 699999.

**Why it matters.** An account outside the five classes has no class to drive its balance and statement. This is a contradiction inside SRC-007.

**Open.** Decision by the qualified accountant. Source: GOV-IMPACT-SRC007 v2, PQ-11.
