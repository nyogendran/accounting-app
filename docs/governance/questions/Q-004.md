# Q-004: Is SRC-001 the requirements baseline, and who confirms its bracketed values?

**Question.** SRC-001 section 3 needs the Finance Manager, Internal Audit and the Managing Director to
sign before the specification is "issued for build" (p.121). Its square brackets mark values
"confirmed before issue", and "none is to be interpreted by the developer" (p.5 to p.6). The extracted
text holds 181 bracket pairs. Leaving out the three signature names in section 3, 178 values are to
be confirmed, 53 of them `[role]`.

**Answer so far** (governance owner, 2026-10-20): Tiqri's internal team shared the specification and
treats it as the signed baseline. Shifan Cader confirmed it.

**Open.**

- The bracketed values. The requirements agent lists each one in a workbook, with the specification's
  own value where it gives one, and the owning role confirms it.
- Confirmation of the baseline by Shifan Cader as product owner, which is to be recorded as a
  decision.
