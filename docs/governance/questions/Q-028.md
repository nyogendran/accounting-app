# Q-028: Owner of statutory data-residency rules

**Question.** Which role owns the statutory data-residency rules for each country?

**What SRC-001 says.**

- "the vendor states where data and backups physically sit" (p.10, S1-2.1.3-c04). Residency law differs by country, and Norway brings EEA data protection rules.
- The authority matrix has no clear owner. The nearest is the tax specialist's "Tax and statutory rules per jurisdiction".

**Why it matters.** To settle in the next change to the authority matrix, after DEC-0007.

**Open.** Decision by the governance owner. Source: REV-01M5DADKQY1VNR1FMN2414KZ58.
