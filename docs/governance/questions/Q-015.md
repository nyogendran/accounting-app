# Q-015: Annual restart of sales tax invoice series

**Question.** Is the annual restart of sales tax invoice series fixed, or a default set per country?

**What SRC-001 says.**

- "restarting at the beginning of each financial year" (p.45, S1-2.4.1-c06) applies one option of the numbering framework (S1-2.1.10-c18, p.21), whose formats are illustrative (S1-2.4.1-c09).

**Assurance recommendation** (REV-01M5DADKQY1VNR1FMN2414KZ58, E4, advisory only): The annual restart is the default for sales tax series, set per country.

**Why it matters.** It sets the numbering configuration for Sri Lanka and Norway.

**Open.** Decision by the tax specialist. Source: REV-01M5DADKQY1VNR1FMN2414KZ58.
