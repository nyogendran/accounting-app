# Q-035: Sales document series: financial-year restart or the entity's choice

**Question.** Which governs a statutory sales series: the financial-year restart, or the entity's choice where an authority prescribes the sequence?

**What SRC-007 says.**

- Each sales series is "restarting at the beginning of each financial year" (p.54, S7-2.4.1-c08).
- "Where the authority prescribes whether a sequence continues across months or restarts, the choice is made once per entity before the first document is issued and can afterwards be changed only with approval and a recorded reason" (p.22, S7-2.1.10-c36).
- SRC-008 (the Sri Lanka field map) says the serial continues from the previous month unless the entity elects to restart monthly or annually.

**Why it matters.** It sets the numbering configuration for Sri Lanka and the other countries.
