# Q-013: Override posting into a closed period

**Question.** May the override route into a hard-closed period be used to correct an entry of that period?

**What SRC-001 says.**

- Corrections are dated in the current open period (S1-2.2.6-c12, c16; p.29).
- "An authorised finance administrator may post an exceptional journal into a hard-closed period through an override route" (p.33, S1-2.2.8-c30). The specification doesn't say what the override may be used for.

**Why it matters.** It decides whether reported figures of a closed period can change through the override.

**Open.** Decision by the qualified accountant. Source: REV-01M5DADKQY1VNR1FMN2414KZ58.
