# Q-003: Which countries, accounting frameworks, currencies and year ends?

**Question.** SRC-001 keeps tax in configuration ("No tax rule is hard-coded in the software", p.68),
lets local and group frameworks differ ("IFRS or GAAP adjustment", p.111), and has each entity
nominate its functional currency (p.34). Its examples include SSCL (p.19, p.68).

**Answer so far** (governance owner, 2026-10-20):

- Multiple countries: Sri Lanka, and other countries such as Norway.
- LKR, with multiple currencies.

**Open.**

- The accounting framework for each country and for the group. Owner: the qualified accountant.
- The financial year end for each entity. Owner: the qualified accountant.
- The group reporting currency. Owner: the qualified accountant.
- The statutory reporting and tax returns in each country. Owner: the tax specialist.
