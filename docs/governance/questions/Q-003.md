# Q-003: Which countries, accounting frameworks, currencies and year ends?

**Question.** SRC-001 keeps tax in configuration ("No tax rule is hard-coded in the software", p.68),
lets local and group frameworks differ ("IFRS or GAAP adjustment", p.111), and has each entity
nominate its functional currency (p.34). Its examples include SSCL (p.19, p.68).

**Answer so far** (governance owner, 2026-10-20):

- Multiple countries: Sri Lanka, and other countries such as Norway.
- LKR, with multiple currencies.

**Answer so far** (governance owner, 2026-10-26): "All countries defined in the template". The
delivered template library received from Finance on 2026-10-21 (to be registered as a source) lists
these jurisdictions:

- Sri Lanka (SL-VAT-01 to 03, SL-NTX-01, SL-TCN-01, SL-TDN-01);
- EU member states and the United Kingdom (EU-VAT-01);
- United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia and the GCC (GCC-VAT-01);
- India (IN-GST-01);
- the United States (US-ST-01).

Its remaining templates (commercial invoice, pro forma, receipt, delivery note, statement) apply to
any country.

**Open.**

- Norway. The answer of 2026-10-20 named Norway, but the library has no Norway template, and
  EU-VAT-01 covers EU member states and the United Kingdom only. Does Norway stay in scope, and if so,
  with which template? Owner: the product owner, with the tax specialist.
- Which of these countries the first releases must support. The release phasing covers Sri Lanka
  first. Owner: the product owner.
- Who confirms the tax and statutory content for the added countries. DEC-0007 names the tax
  specialist for Sri Lanka and Norway only; any other country needs a named tax specialist, which is a
  change to the authority matrix. Owner: the governance owner.
- The accounting framework for each country and for the group. Owner: the qualified accountant.
- The financial year end for each entity. Owner: the qualified accountant.
- The group reporting currency. Owner: the qualified accountant.
- The statutory reporting and tax returns in each country. Owner: the tax specialist.
