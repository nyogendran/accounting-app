# Q-056: Who creates a tenant, its first entity and its first administrator

**Question.** How does a tenant come into existence, with its first entity and its first
administrator, and who may do it? After that, what may the person or service that created it still
reach inside the tenant?

**What SRC-007 says.**

- "Roles are built by the entity's administrator, not fixed by the vendor" (p.8, S7-2.1.2-c01).
- No clause found in the SRC-007 clause index describes creating a tenant or its first administrator
  (searched for onboarding, provisioning, first administrator and creating a tenant or entity).

**Why it matters.** Every foundation capability that relies on an administrator (GOV-SCOPE-INCR-001 v1,
CAP-01 to CAP-04) has no starting state without it. Whoever creates tenants can reach into them, which
links this question to Q-031 v3 reading (c), Tiqri as operator, and to the scope's point SEC-2.

**Answer so far** (governance owner, as security owner, 2026-10-28): "There should be an owner or
tenant administrator who should be able to manage tenants (provisioning/onboarding clients)."

- A named administrative role creates tenants and onboards clients; tenants are not created by an
  unattended sign-up.
- "Owner or tenant administrator" can be read in two ways, and the reading sets the isolation
  boundary:
  - (a) a platform-level owner role (Tiqri as operator of the product) provisions every tenant;
  - (b) the administrator of a subscribing firm's tenant onboards its client organisations as new
    tenants, consistent with Q-031 v3 (a firm keeps the books of client organisations, each its own
    tenant).
- Still open: which reading applies, or both; how the first administrator of a new tenant is named;
  and what the provisioning role can reach inside a tenant after creating it (security owner). Whether
  provisioning is Tiqri's service or self-service for subscribers is the product owner's.

**Open.** The access and control question is the security owner's. Whether Tiqri provisions tenants,
or customers sign up themselves, is the product owner's. Raised from REV-01M61MRRKX52P8XHG3NZXSPCZJ
finding B1.
