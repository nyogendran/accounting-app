# Q-057: What happens at a capacity limit, and who may raise it

**Question.** When a tenant reaches a configured capacity figure, such as users, entities or journal
lines, does the system warn only or refuse? Who may raise the figure: the tenant's own administrator or
Tiqri as operator of the shared platform? Is a cap on named users a licence matter (2.16.5) and not a
capacity one?

**What SRC-007 says.**

- "No figure in this section is a licence cap, a tier boundary or a limit the software enforces against
  the entity that bought it" (p.134, S7-2.16.1-c33).
- "An entity that outgrows a delivered default raises it itself, as above: a configuration value with
  an effective date" (p.134, S7-2.16.1-c34).
- "An entity approaching the tested ceiling is told before it reaches it, not after" (p.134,
  S7-2.16.1-c35).
- "The figures in 2.16.1 are technical capacity, not commercial entitlement" (p.136, S7-2.16.5-c23).

**Why it matters.** GOV-SCOPE-INCR-001 v1 (CAP-06, EXC-21) counts named users and entities against a
configurable limit. Without an answer, one team would build a hard cap and another a warning, and the
acceptance criteria would be guesses. On a shared platform, raising a figure affects other tenants
(Q-038).

**Answer so far** (governance owner, as security owner and tech lead, 2026-10-28): "Tenant admin should
be able to increase the cap limit."

- The tenant's own administrator may raise a capacity figure, as S7-2.16.1-c34 reads ("An entity that
  outgrows a delivered default raises it itself").
- Still open: whether the system warns only or refuses at a figure (product owner); whether a tenant
  may also lower a figure, and whether any figure has a platform ceiling that only Tiqri as operator may
  raise on the shared platform (tech lead, with Q-038); and whether named-user licensing (2.16.5) is
  separate from these figures (product owner, Q-039).

**Answer so far, second** (governance owner, as security owner and tech lead, 2026-10-28), on
REV-01M61R5FTD3DD5RK7G0TH7G0NS findings NF11 and NF12:

- NF11, which role holds the permission to raise a figure: option (a), "add a tenant-level
  administrator role that holds the permission". The other options offered were (b) each entity's
  Administrator, for that entity's figures only, and (c) an ordinary permission any role may be granted.
- NF12, whether a tenant may raise a figure without any upper bound: "Not sure whether the Tiqri or
  Platform Admin should be able to increase the upper bound." Read as: an upper bound above the
  tenant's own figures is expected, and who may raise it (Tiqri as operator, or a platform
  administrator role) is open.
- What SRC-007 says on the bound, checked on the rendered PDF: "Limits are set per tenant and per
  entity, never globally" (p.133, S7-2.16.1-c25); "No figure in this section is a licence cap, a tier
  boundary or a limit the software enforces against the entity that bought it" (p.134, S7-2.16.1-c33);
  "An entity approaching the tested ceiling is told before it reaches it, not after, and a capacity
  review is raised" (p.134, S7-2.16.1-c35); "Where a client needs capacity beyond the ceiling, the
  ceiling is re-proved at the new level by the same load tests used at acceptance" (p.134,
  S7-2.16.1-c38).
- Still open: whether a bound exists that the software enforces, given c33; who raises it; and
  whether it is the tested ceiling of c35 and c38 (tech lead, with the product owner).

**Answer so far, third** (governance owner, as tech lead, 2026-10-28), on NF12: "The upper bound on
capacity Tiqri's load-tested ceiling with warning."

- The upper bound above a tenant's own figures is Tiqri's load-tested ceiling (S7-2.16.1-c35, c38).
  A tenant approaching it is warned; it is not refused. This matches "No figure in this section is a
  licence cap, a tier boundary or a limit the software enforces against the entity that bought it"
  (p.134, S7-2.16.1-c33).
- Moving the ceiling is Tiqri's, by re-proving it in load tests for that client (c38).
- Still open: whether the system warns only or refuses at a tenant's own figure (product owner); whether
  a tenant may lower a figure; and licensing (product owner, Q-039).

**Open.** The product behaviour and licensing are the product owner's. Platform capacity is the tech
lead's. Who may raise a figure is the security owner's. Raised from REV-01M61MRRKX52P8XHG3NZXSPCZJ
finding B3.
