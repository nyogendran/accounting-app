# Q-023: Clauses addressed to a vendor

**Question.** How is each clause that asks a vendor to state, quote or answer something handled, now that TIQACC is Tiqri's own product (Q-001)?

**What SRC-001 says.**

- Examples: "Encryption in transit and at rest, with the standard stated in the vendor response" (p.9, S1-2.1.3-c03), and "the vendor states where data and backups physically sit" (p.10, S1-2.1.3-c04).
- Also resting on the vendor relationship: "at no extra charge" (S1-2.16.1-c06) and the handover deliverables (S1-2.16.3-c01 to c04).
- The obligations themselves, such as encryption and data residency, stand. Only the "vendor states" parts need an internal answer or a decision to drop them.

**Why it matters.** Owners: the product owner for scope and commercial terms; the security owner with the tech lead for the encryption standard and hosting locations.

**Open.** Decision by the product owner. Source: REV-01M5DADKQY1VNR1FMN2414KZ58.
