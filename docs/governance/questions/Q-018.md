# Q-018: Tax registration references on documents for unregistered customers

**Question.** What does the acceptance test on tax registration references for unregistered customers forbid?

**What SRC-001 says.**

- "An unregistered customer cannot be issued a document carrying a tax registration reference." (p.48, S1-2.4.8-c02).
- Yet a tax invoice may go to any customer where the law requires one (S1-2.4.1-c01). The unregistered case follows a per-jurisdiction rule (S1-2.4.2-c05), and a tax invoice carries the "Supplier name, address and tax registration number" (p.46, 2.4.3). The non-tax invoice carries "No tax registration reference where its inclusion is not permitted" (PDF p.46, 2.4.3; the extraction interleaves this line).

**Readings.**

- (A) The test refers to the customer's own registration number only.
- (B) The test refers to any registration number where its inclusion is not permitted.

**Why it matters.** It decides what the invoice templates print for unregistered customers.
