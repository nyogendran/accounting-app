# Q-012: Hard close with an unapproved bank reconciliation

**Question.** Can a period be hard-closed with an unapproved bank reconciliation, under a formally accepted exception?

**What SRC-001 says.**

- "A period cannot be hard-closed while a bank reconciliation for that period is unapproved or shows an unexplained difference." (p.62, S1-2.6.6-c21), with no exception route.
- "A period cannot be hard-closed until every in-scope reconciliation is approved, or an exception is formally accepted by [role] with a reason recorded." (p.101, S1-2.12.3-c06). Bank and cash are in scope (S1-2.12.1-c07).

**Why it matters.** It decides whether the close checklist has an exception route for bank.

**Open.** Decision by the qualified accountant. Source: REV-01M5DADKQY1VNR1FMN2414KZ58.
