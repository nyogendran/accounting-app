# Q-016: Standalone credit and debit notes

**Question.** Are credit and debit notes without an original invoice allowed in each country, and if so with what tax treatment and limit?

**What SRC-001 says.**

- "Against a named invoice or standalone" (p.38, S1-2.3.3-c10).
- "Every credit and debit note references the original document number and inherits its tax treatment" (p.48, S1-2.4.6-c03), and its value is capped at the original's. It is also unclear whether the cap applies to debit notes (S1-2.4.1-c05).

**Why it matters.** The qualified accountant decides the treatment of standalone credits with the tax specialist.

**Open.** Decision by the tax specialist. Source: REV-01M5DADKQY1VNR1FMN2414KZ58.
