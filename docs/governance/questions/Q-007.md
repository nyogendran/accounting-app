# Q-007: May dimensions change on a posted entry after approval

**Question.** May the dimensions of a posted, approved entry change, and if so how?

**What SRC-001 says.**

- "No screen, import, API call or administrator action can change its value, account, date, dimensions or party" (SRC-001 p.29, S1-2.2.6-c10).
- "Narrative, internal reference or dimension changes that alter no value and no account may be permitted after approval" (p.29, S1-2.2.6-c14). The two conflict for dimensions only.

**Readings.**

- (A) c10 governs: a wrong dimension is corrected by reversal and re-entry.
- (B) c14 is a per-entity exception: posted lines become editable in their dimensions.
- (C) c14 is met by a system-generated, linked reclassification entry between dimension values on the same account, so c10's lock stays intact.

**Assurance recommendation** (REV-01M5DADKQY1VNR1FMN2414KZ58, E4, advisory only): (C), with the qualified accountant also deciding whether it applies in closed or filed periods.

**Why it matters.** Whether posted lines can ever change in place is an early data-model decision.

**Open.** Decision by the qualified accountant. Source: REV-01M5DADKQY1VNR1FMN2414KZ58.
