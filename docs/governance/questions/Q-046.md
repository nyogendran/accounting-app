# Q-046: No calendar-year reset rule

**Question.** Should the numbering reset rule offer a calendar-year reset?

**What SRC-007 says.**

- The reset rule is "Never, annually at the financial year end, or at the start of each period — set per series" (p.22, S7-2.1.10-c18). The Sri Lanka serial takes its year from the calendar year of issue (SRC-008).

**Why it matters.** A financial year that is not the calendar year would otherwise reset the series at the wrong time.

**Open.** Decision by the tax specialist, with the tech lead. Source: GOV-IMPACT-SRC007 v2, PQ-17.
