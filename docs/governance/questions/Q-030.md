# Q-030: What is the expected credit loss provision policy?

**Question.** On what basis, and at what loss rates, is the expected credit loss provision computed?

**What SRC-001 says.**

- "Expected credit loss is computed on a provision matrix maintained by" Finance (SRC-001 p.41,
  S1-2.3.10-c01). SRC-001 settles who maintains the matrix; the policy and the loss rates are open
  (REV-01M5EP1W4DYPM707EB79AYEYV1).

**Why it matters.** The policy depends on the accounting framework (Q-003), and receivables is in the
proposed release 1.

**Open.** Decision by the qualified accountant. Source: the requirements steward's handoff for
