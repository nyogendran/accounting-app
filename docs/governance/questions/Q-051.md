# Q-051: Who the Sri Lanka non-tax invoice is for

**Question.** Is SRC-008's non-tax invoice template SL-NTX-01 for an unregistered supplier, an unregistered purchaser, or both?

**What SRC-007 says.**

- SRC-008 sends a supply to an unregistered customer to SL-NTX-01, and also describes SL-NTX-01 as for suppliers not registered for VAT (template library p.5).

**Why it matters.** It decides what the template prints, with Q-018.

**Open.** Decision by the tax specialist. Source: GOV-IMPACT-SRC007 v2, PQ-23.
