# Q-017: Cancelling an issued invoice

**Question.** When may an issued invoice be cancelled rather than credited, and what entry does cancellation raise?

**What SRC-001 says.**

- "Cancellation is permitted only before payment and before the tax return for that period is filed" (p.48, S1-2.4.6-c02), and an invoice can reach the state cancelled (S1-2.3.2-c05).
- But the correction table sends issued invoices to credit or debit notes (S1-2.2.6-c17), and the receivables entry table (2.3.12) has no row for cancelling an invoice that has already posted.

**Why it matters.** The tax specialist decides whether a tax invoice may be cancelled in each country; the qualified accountant decides the reversing entry and its period.

**Open.** Decision by the tax specialist. Source: REV-01M5DADKQY1VNR1FMN2414KZ58.
