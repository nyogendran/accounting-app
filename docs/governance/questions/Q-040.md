# Q-040: Two decimal places in the journal upload template

**Question.** Are upload amounts fixed at two decimal places, or set by each currency's minor unit?

**What SRC-007 says.**

- The template fixes debit and credit amounts at "Decimal, [2] places" (p.35, S7-2.2.4-c49, c50). Some currencies have no or three minor units.

**Why it matters.** It affects every journal upload in a currency without two decimals.

**Answer so far** (governance owner, as tech lead, 2026-10-28): "should be configurable by country's
currency configuration."

- Read as: the number of decimal places in an amount is set by the currency's configuration, not
  fixed at two, so the bracketed "[2] places" (p.35) becomes the currency's own number of places.

**Open.** Confirmation by the qualified accountant, who owns this question; and whether the
currency's places also govern stored amounts, rounding and tax computation, or only the upload
template. Decision by the qualified accountant, with the tech lead. Source: GOV-IMPACT-SRC007 v2, PQ-10.
