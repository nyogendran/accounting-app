# Q-038: Capacity targets for release 1

**Question.** Which capacity figures are release 1 built and load-tested against, and is the single-entity customer figure 5,000 or 25,000?

**What SRC-007 says.**

- "The system is built against the figures below and proved against the ceiling in load testing before acceptance" (p.133, S7-2.16.1-c07). The ceiling is set for a large multinational group.
- The single-entity customer figure is 5,000 (S7-2.16.1-c17); SRC-008 gives 25,000 and says: Build against the "Multinational group" column ('Volume and capacity' r3).

**Why it matters.** Proving a multinational ceiling is a large part of release-1 acceptance for one Sri Lanka entity.

**Answer so far** (governance owner, as tech lead, 2026-10-28): "Let's go with single-entity customer
figure 5,000 (assuming this is the customers loads)."

- The 5,000 is V2.2's figure (p.133, S7-2.16.1-c17). The specification overrides SRC-008, so the
  pack's 25,000 is not used.
- On the reading of "customers": the row "Customers" sits with the master-data rows (accounts in the
  chart, stock items, employees), so it is the number of customer records held, not a transaction
  load. The monthly loads are separate rows of the same column, for example "Journal lines posted per
  month" 200,000 (c08) and "Customer invoices issued per month" 100,000 (c09).

**Open.**

- Whether the whole single-entity column applies to release-1 acceptance, or only the customer
  figure. The column includes one legal entity (c14), 200,000 journal lines and 100,000 invoices a
  month, 125 named users and 50 at peak (c21, c22).
- Whether the figures are per tenant. Tenants are organisations, and Tiqri may manage several client
  accounts (Q-031 v2), so the platform as a whole carries the sum of its tenants.
- Confirmation by the product owner, who owns this question, with the tech lead. Source: GOV-IMPACT-SRC007 v2, PQ-08.
