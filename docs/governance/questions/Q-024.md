# Q-024: Service levels TIQACC commits to tenants

**Question.** What support SLA, availability, recovery point and recovery time, and response times does TIQACC commit to its tenants?

**What SRC-001 says.**

- "Severity-based response and resolution times stated in the SLA" (p.119, S1-2.16.1-c05). No SLA document exists.
- The targets are workbook values 16, 96, 153, 169 and 175 to 177. The severity levels should match "No module is accepted with an open critical or high defect" (p.120, S1-2.16.4-c03).

**Why it matters.** The product owner decides, with the tech lead confirming feasibility.

**Open.** Decision by the product owner. Source: REV-01M5DADKQY1VNR1FMN2414KZ58.
