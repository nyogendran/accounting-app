# Q-014: Chart-of-accounts naming standard

**Question.** What is the naming standard for account names?

**What SRC-001 says.**

- "Follows the naming standard in 2.2.2; abbreviations and personal shorthand are not permitted" (p.23, S1-2.2.1-c05), and "Unique; follows the naming standard" (p.24, S1-2.2.2-c08). Section 2.2.2 contains no standard.

**Why it matters.** The chart is created by the qualified accountant in the system (Q-005); the standard is a missing input to that.

**Open.** Decision by the qualified accountant. Source: REV-01M5DADKQY1VNR1FMN2414KZ58.
