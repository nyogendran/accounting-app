# Q-009: Separation of preparing, reviewing and approving a reconciliation

**Question.** How are preparing, reviewing and approving a reconciliation separated, and can that separation be released for a small team?

**What SRC-001 says.**

- 2.1.2 lets this separation be released: "including preparing and reviewing the same reconciliation" (p.9, S1-2.1.2-c17). 2.12 states "self-review is blocked" (p.101, S1-2.12.3-c04), and its acceptance test accepts a released case flagged as self-reviewed (S1-2.12.5-c03).
- Also "the user who prepares a reconciliation cannot approve it" (p.67, S1-2.6.16-c13), with separate reviewed and approved states, and "a user cannot approve a document they raised" (S1-2.1.2-c16) may or may not cover reconciliations.

**Assurance recommendation** (REV-01M5DADKQY1VNR1FMN2414KZ58, E4, advisory only): A single second step (review is the approval), releasable for every reconciliation including bank, with S1-2.1.2-c16 not applied to reconciliations. The same question applies to the three-user tax return workflow (S1-2.7.4-c06).

**Why it matters.** If approval is a third step the preparer can never take, a one-person team cannot finish a reconciliation.

**Open.** Decision by the qualified accountant. Source: REV-01M5DADKQY1VNR1FMN2414KZ58.
