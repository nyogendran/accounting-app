# Q-050: Inconsistent illustrations in SRC-008

**Question.** Correct SRC-008's inconsistent illustrations before any of them becomes test data.

**What SRC-007 says.**

- The receipt and statement specimens omit the levy (969,960.00 against 994,209.00), the credit-note number differs between documents, the ageing appears reversed, and the stated count of fourteen templates is fifteen (GOV-IMPACT-SRC007 v2, section 5).

**Why it matters.** Specimens become expected results in tests.

**Open.** Correction by the qualified accountant. Source: GOV-IMPACT-SRC007 v2, PQ-22.
