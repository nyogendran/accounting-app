# Q-010: Day count for pro-rata depreciation and the worked example

**Question.** What day count and leap-year treatment does the depreciation start rule "pro-rata by days" use, and must the worked example be corrected?

**What SRC-001 says.**

- The worked example sets a "start rule pro-rata by days" (p.78, S1-2.8.3-c26) from [1 October], yet charges 60,000 in year 1: exactly 3/12 of the annual 240,000. Actual days (92/365) would give 60,493.15. The example also assumes a 31 December year end, which Q-003 leaves open.
- The figures are labelled illustrative, but the acceptance test relies on the start rule (S1-2.8.16-c02).

**Assurance recommendation** (REV-01M5DADKQY1VNR1FMN2414KZ58, E4, advisory only): State the day count and leap-year treatment, and correct the rule or the figures before the example becomes test data.

**Why it matters.** The example will otherwise become a wrong expected result in a test.

**Open.** Decision by the qualified accountant. Source: REV-01M5DADKQY1VNR1FMN2414KZ58.
