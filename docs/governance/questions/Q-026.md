# Q-026: Auditor access to individual pay

**Question.** May the auditor role see individual pay amounts?

**What SRC-001 says.**

- The auditor may "Read every module and export any report" (p.9, S1-2.1.2-c27), with access to every module and audit log (S1-2.11.6-c01).
- "Payroll amounts are visible only to named payroll users and [role]" (p.98, S1-2.10.14-c01; workbook value 156).

**Why it matters.** The security owner advises. Decide together with workbook value 156.

**Open.** Decision by the product owner. Source: REV-01M5DADKQY1VNR1FMN2414KZ58.
