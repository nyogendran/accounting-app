# Q-005: What can Finance supply for Annexure B?

**Question.** SRC-001 lists "Annexure B — current chart of accounts, monthly transaction volumes by
module, and our existing tax invoice and non-tax invoice formats" (p.121). The annexure is not in the
repository.

**Answer so far** (governance owner, 2026-10-20):

- **Chart of accounts:** the qualified accountant creates it in the built system, following SRC-001
  2.2.1: "The chart of accounts is the entity's own, maintained by Finance in the system" (p.23). The
  current chart is therefore needed only if data is migrated (Q-006).
- **Monthly volumes and invoice formats:** to be gathered from Finance.

**Open.**

- **Monthly volumes**, typical and peak, for each area: entities, currencies, users, customers,
  invoices, receipts, suppliers, bills, payments, bank lines, assets, stock and payroll.
- **Invoice formats:** a blank or redacted sample of each tax invoice and non-tax invoice layout in
  use, per country.

Totals and blank or redacted documents only. No production financial or personal data enters the
repository.
