# Q-036: Two corrupted rows in SRC-007

**Question.** Confirm the intended wording of two rows that are corrupted in SRC-007.

**What SRC-007 says.**

- The translatable-records table reads "Customers 25,000" (PDF p.19, S7-2.1.8-c36). V1 read "Customers Name and trading name, where the customer is known by more than one script".
- The configuration-alerts table reads "Legal entities 1 Numbering series" (PDF p.130, S7-2.15.8-c22). V1 read "Legal entities Consolidation structure Numbering series".
- Both look like figures pasted from the capacity table in 2.16.1. The orchestrator confirmed both on the rendered PDF pages.

**Why it matters.** Requirements cannot be written from a corrupted row.

**Open.** Confirmation by the product owner, and a corrected specification from Finance. Source: GOV-IMPACT-SRC007 v2, PQ-05.
