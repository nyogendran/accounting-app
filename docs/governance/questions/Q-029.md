# Q-029: How do payroll, depreciation and other sub-ledger postings reach the ledger before their modules exist?

**Version 5** corrects v4 after the review REV-01M5W4F011JE50FZ3VMWD039DG:

- option C has two readings under V2.2;
- corporate income tax raises the same question under every option;
- the wider scope of the question is restored.

The baseline is SRC-007, V2.2 (DEC-0008).

**Question.** The phasing proposes payroll and fixed assets for release 2. Until then, which route
should payroll and depreciation postings take to the ledger?

**Scope.** The phasing's decision D-INT-1 covers all of these:

- payroll postings;
- fixed-asset events, including depreciation;
- leases, if any;
- the payroll tax returns built from payroll;
- corporate income tax postings, if 2.7.6 is not in release 1;
- opening fixed-asset and payroll balances (with Q-045 and Q-006).

**Answer these first.** The options below depend on them:

1. **Q-032:** do the enforcement levels govern, or does the attribute table's blanket block?
2. **Q-033:** what level does the first entity start at? The defaults are set by organisation. Read
   for Tiqri as a whole, the evidence points to Enforced; read for the first entity alone, it may be
   Advisory. Is choosing a lower level at set-up a "lowering", which needs approval and is reported on
   the 2.15.8 panel?
3. **Q-034:** can a control flag be set later on an account that already has history?
4. **Q-047:** if manual journals reach a control account, does the rule that agreements "must be nil"
   govern?

**What SRC-007 says, whatever those answers are.**

- "No user journal is acceptable as a substitute" (SRC-007 p.8, S7-2-c02). Payroll is a row of what
  the system posts "without a manual journal" (p.4, S7-0-c10).
- Depreciation "must be auto-calculated by the system, not by a spreadsheet" (p.90, S7-2.8.3-c01).
- Sub-ledger agreements "are auto-computed by the system continuously and must be nil" (p.114,
  S7-2.12.1-c01). The rows for asset and payroll balances (c05, c06) have nothing to agree to before the
  modules exist.
- At Enforced, "A manual journal to a control account is refused. Only the owning sub-ledger posts to
  it, and the balance is reproducible from that sub-ledger at any past date" (p.27, S7-2.2.1-c57).
  Activating a module proposes raising the level to Enforced (c63).

**Options.**

- **(A) Interim manual journals until release 2.**
  - If Q-032 settles for the levels and Q-033 gives Advisory or below, or an approved lowering, the
    journals can post with a reason and appear on an exception report (S7-2.2.1-c56). Neither a change
    request nor interim non-control accounts is needed.
  - Otherwise the V1 position stands: relax the block by a change request, or post to interim
    non-control accounts and move the balances at release 2. The flag cannot change on an account with
    history (S7-2.2.2-c47, c51; Q-034).
  - Either way, an interim journal is not an accepted substitute (S7-2-c02). The agreements cannot run
    for payroll and fixed assets in release 1. History before the level is raised is not reproducible
    from a sub-ledger.
- **(B) Payroll and the fixed-asset core in release 1.** This avoids the payroll and fixed-asset points
  above, and makes release 1 larger.
- **(C) Payroll by interface from the payroll system in use today; fixed assets in release 1.** V2.2
  supports two readings:
  - **Reading 1.** Where the payroll module is not active, "the external system takes its place and
    feeds TIQACC through this interface" (p.11, S7-2.1.6-c14). It is then the owning sub-ledger, and C
    works at Enforced.
  - **Reading 2.** Only TIQACC's payroll module owns the payroll control accounts (S7-2.2.1-c57), so C
    needs the payroll control type below Enforced. It then has a control cost like option A.

  The qualified accountant chooses the reading, with the product owner. Also open: Q-022 (an external
  payroll feeding TIQACC); how interface postings (S7-2.1.6-c15) are treated; and how the payroll
  agreement works without payroll runs.

**Corporate income tax, under every option.** If 2.7.6 waits for release 2, the current tax charge
("Dr Income tax expense · Cr Income tax payable", p.84, S7-2.7.6-c07) and instalments (c08) post to
income tax payable, a tax control account (S7-2.2.1-c06). That raises the option A question under B
and C as well. Either 2.7.6 comes into release 1, or its interim postings follow Q-032 and Q-033.
2.7.6 also holds "Turnover levy accrued" (c10), which release 1 needs if a levy applies (Q-042). This is
phasing decision D-2.7.6, for the tax specialist with the qualified accountant.

**Assurance advice** (REV-01M5EP1W4DYPM707EB79AYEYV1, E4, advisory only): consider C, on condition that
the qualified accountant confirms interface postings count as sub-ledger postings. Under V2.2 that is
reading 1.

**Why it matters.** It sets the size and order of release 1, and whether a control is lowered or
relaxed.

**Open.** Decision by the product owner, with the qualified accountant (control accounts and the reading
of option C) and the tax specialist (payroll tax and income tax), after Q-032, Q-033, Q-034 and Q-047.
