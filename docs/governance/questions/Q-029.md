# Q-029: How do payroll, depreciation and other sub-ledger postings reach the ledger before their modules exist?

**Question.** The first proposed phasing put payroll and fixed assets in release 2, with payroll and
depreciation posted by approved manual journal until then. The assurance review
(REV-01M5EP1W4DYPM707EB79AYEYV1, finding 1) showed that this conflicts with a release-1 control. Which
route should release 1 take?

**What SRC-001 says.**

- "No user journal is acceptable as a substitute" (SRC-001 p.8, S1-2-c02).
- "Depreciation must be auto-calculated by the system, not by a spreadsheet." (p.77, S1-2.8.3-c01)
- Control accounts "accept sub-ledger postings only; manual journals to them are blocked" (p.23,
  S1-2.2.1-c06). That covers receivables, payables, inventory, payroll, fixed asset, tax and bank.
- The control flag "cannot be changed while the account carries a balance" (p.24, S1-2.2.2-c12), so
  leaving payroll or fixed-asset accounts unflagged in release 1 cannot be quietly corrected later.

**Options.**

- **(A) Interim manual journals.** This has a cost either way.
  - (i) Relax the release-1 control-account block, through a change request (SRC-001 is unsigned, Q-004).
  - (ii) Post to interim non-control accounts, which can never be flagged once they have transactions
    (S1-2.2.2-c16). At release 2 the balances move to new control accounts. SRC-001 supports two readings
    of how, and the qualified accountant chooses:
    - **R1:** the manual-journal block (S1-2.2.1-c21) governs, so the balances arrive through the
      sub-ledger.
    - **R2:** the specific reclassification rule (S1-2.2.2-c16) and its controlled tool (S1-2.2.2-c18,
      p.25) allow one approved reclassification.
  - Payroll tax returns, corporate income tax instalments and leases rely on the same route.
- **(B) Payroll and fixed assets in release 1.** This removes the conflict, and makes release 1 larger.
- **(C) Mixed.** Payroll results are fed from the payroll system in use today through the integration
  interface, as SRC-001 allows for entities without the payroll module (S1-2.1.6-c14; depends on Q-022
  and Q-006). The fixed-asset core moves into release 1, because SRC-001 gives depreciation no interim
  route. The qualified accountant must confirm two things:
  - that interface postings (S1-2.1.6-c15) count as sub-ledger postings;
  - how the payroll sub-ledger agreement (S1-2.12.1-c06) works without payroll run totals in release 1.

The phasing (`docs/requirements/release-phasing.yaml` v3, D-INT-1 and `variants`) lists what moves
under each option.

**Assurance recommendation** (REV-01M5EP1W4DYPM707EB79AYEYV1, E4, advisory only): consider (C).

**Why it matters.** It sets the size and order of release 1, and whether a control is relaxed.

**Open.** Decision by the product owner, with the qualified accountant (the control-account question)
and the tax specialist (payroll tax and income tax returns).
