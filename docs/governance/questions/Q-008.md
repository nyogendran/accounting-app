# Q-008: Suspense balance at hard close

**Question.** Must suspense be nil before a period is hard-closed, or may explained and approved items remain?

**What SRC-001 says.**

- "Suspense must be nil before a period can be closed." (p.60, S1-2.6.3-c15).
- "Suspense must be nil, or every remaining item explained and approved, before a period can be hard-closed." (p.64, S1-2.6.11-c10). The bank acceptance test S1-2.6.17-c10 (p.67) agrees with the second.

**Assurance recommendation** (REV-01M5DADKQY1VNR1FMN2414KZ58, E4, advisory only): S1-2.6.11-c10 governs, because "A requirement is specified once and referenced elsewhere" (p.6) and 2.6.11 is where suspense is specified. S1-2.6.3-c15 is then read as a pointer to it.

**Why it matters.** It decides whether an explained suspense balance can survive the hard close.

**Open.** Decision by the qualified accountant. Source: REV-01M5DADKQY1VNR1FMN2414KZ58.
