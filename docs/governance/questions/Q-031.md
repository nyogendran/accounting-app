# Q-031: What is one tenant in TIQACC?

**Question.** TIQACC is Tiqri's multi-tenant SaaS product (Q-001). Is a tenant one legal entity, one
group of entities, or one customer organisation with several groups?

**What SRC-001 says.** SRC-001 uses "tenant" once (S1-2.1.1-c01) and does not define it. Its group
features (2.14) work across entities. The assurance review (REV-01M5EP1W4DYPM707EB79AYEYV1, finding
12) found that earlier drafts read "entity" as "tenant" without any source; that reading is removed.

**Why it matters.** It sets the data-isolation boundary (BO-08) and how consolidation crosses
entities. That is an early architecture and security decision.

**Answer so far** (governance owner, as tech lead and security owner, 2026-10-28): "Tenant is per
organization, example Tiqri, however Tiqri should be able to manage multiple client accounts."

- A tenant is one organisation, such as Tiqri, which may hold several legal entities.
- "Manage multiple client accounts" can be read more than one way, and the reading sets the isolation
  boundary:
  - (a) Tiqri keeps the books of client organisations, each client its own tenant, with Tiqri's users
    given access across those tenants;
  - (b) clients are entities inside Tiqri's own tenant;
  - (c) Tiqri, as the operator of the product, administers its customers' tenants (set-up, support,
    subscriptions under 2.16.5) without access to their books.

  Which is meant is open.

**Answer so far** (governance owner, 2026-10-28, second answer): "Tiqri can keep the books for client
organizations. Additionally, this is going to be a product which can be subscribed by Audit Firm or
Clients who can manage their and other clients books."

- Reading (a) applies: a firm keeps the books of client organisations, each client its own tenant,
  and the firm's users work across those tenants.
- The subscriber is not only Tiqri. An audit firm, or a client organisation, may subscribe and manage
  its own books and the books of other clients.

**Open.**

- How a firm gains access to a client's tenant: who owns the client's tenant and its data, who grants
  and withdraws the firm's access, and how that access is logged. Owner: the security owner, with the
  product owner.
- Whether a firm's staff act in a client's books under the client's roles (2.1.2) or under roles of
  their own. V2.2's auditor role reads "every module" and exports "any report" (p.9, S7-2.1.2-c27); a
  firm keeping the books needs more than that. Owner: the product owner.
- Whether an audit firm may keep the books of a client it also audits, and whether the product must
  enforce any restriction. This is a professional and regulatory question, not a design choice; the
  agents assume nothing. Owner: the qualified accountant.
- How subscriptions (2.16.5) apply to a firm and its clients: who subscribes and pays for a client's
  tenant (Q-039). Owner: the product owner.
- Confirmation of the whole answer by the product owner, who owns this question, with the tech lead
  and the security owner.
